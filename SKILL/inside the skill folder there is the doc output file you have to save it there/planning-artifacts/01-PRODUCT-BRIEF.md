## 1. The Idea (One Sentence)

SkillForge AI is an AI-powered, two-sided career-readiness and recruitment platform that bridges the gap between what candidates *claim* to know and what they can *actually do* — through realistic career simulations, explainable AI grading, and a shared skill intelligence layer connecting job seekers to recruiters.

---

## 2. The Problem We Are Solving

### For Candidates / Job Seekers:
- Resumes and certificates are weak, ungameable signals of real capability.
- Candidates have no realistic way to simulate what a job actually requires before applying.
- Assessment feedback is binary (pass/fail), giving no actionable direction for improvement.
- Career direction is unclear — candidates do not know which path fits their current skills.

### For HR / Recruiters:
- Resume screening is noisy and gameable — keyword-optimized CVs do not indicate real competence.
- Generic MCQ assessments are easy to cheat and do not map to real job performance.
- Recruiters have no explainable, defensible way to justify shortlisting decisions to hiring managers.
- Side-by-side comparison of candidates on consistent criteria is painfully manual.

---

## 3. The Solution

SkillForge AI creates a closed career-readiness loop:

  Assess -> Identify Gaps -> Simulate -> Grade (Explainable AI) -> Improve -> Re-assess -> Verified Profile -> Match -> Hire

Both sides share the same Skill Intelligence Layer — one unified data source driving both candidate growth and recruiter intelligence.

---

## 4. Target Users

| User | Core Need |
|---|---|
| Students / Early-career candidates | Know if they are job-ready before applying |
| Experienced job seekers / career switchers | Prove capability beyond resume history |
| HR Recruiters | Find genuinely skilled candidates, fast |
| Hiring Managers | Review evidence-backed candidate shortlists |
| Platform Admins (internal) | Maintain content, integrity, access |

---

## 5. Core Differentiators

1. Career Simulations — Role-realistic scenarios, not generic quizzes
2. Explainable AI Grading — Every score shows why, not just what
3. Shared Skill Layer — Same data powers both candidate profiles and recruiter matching
4. Closed Loop — Assessment + Learning + Job Matching, all connected

---

## 6. Business Model

- Freemium: Candidate side is free with usage limits at scale
- Primary Revenue: HR/Recruiter paid subscription tier
- Growth Engine: More assessments -> richer skill data -> higher recruiter value -> paid adoption

---

## 7. What This Is NOT

- Not a full LMS or course platform
- Not an autonomous hiring decision engine (humans always decide)
- Not a general-purpose job board competing on listing volume
- Not a resume keyword-ranking system disguised as AI

---

## 8. Current Stage

- Pre-development — concept validated, dev team available
- No existing users, no live MVP, no real analytics data yet
- API setup and domain/infrastructure pending before build begins
- India as initial pilot market (assumption — pending founder confirmation)

---

## 9. MVP Focus

Prove the closed candidate loop + basic explainable HR matching end-to-end for a limited career track set.

MVP Must-Haves:
- Candidate: onboarding -> assessment -> simulation -> explainable career readiness score
- HR: job creation -> AI candidate matching -> explainable match score -> shortlisting
